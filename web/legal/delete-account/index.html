Account & Data Deletion

You have the right to request permanent deletion of your no5ocial account and all associated workspace data at any time.

Option 1: In-App Immediate Deletion (Recommended)

If you still have the no5ocial app installed on your device:

  1. Open no5ocial and navigate to the Settings tab.
  2. Scroll down to the Workspace Storage & Danger Zone section.
  3. Tap Permanently Delete Account.
  4. Review the confirmation dialog and confirm. Your session, workspace profile, linked channels, draft posts, and cloud documents are permanently deleted immediately.

Option 2: Web Deletion Request (App Uninstalled)

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot access your device, submit a web deletion request directly to our security & privacy team:

Email our designated Data Protection Officer at:

privacy@shaadow.io

Please include the Google account email address associated with your no5ocial workspace. Deletion requests are processed within 48 hours.

Email Deletion Request

What Data Is Deleted Upon Request?

  • User Profile: Your unique identifier, email, display name, and avatar URL are removed from active databases.
  • Social Account Tokens: All OAuth tokens, refresh tokens, and permissions for Instagram, YouTube, LinkedIn, X, and Threads are immediately revoked and erased.
  • Content Posts & Drafts: All captions, media references, scheduled publish jobs, and upload histories are permanently deleted.
  • Organization Memberships: Your workspace roles, invites, and team mappings are unlinked.

Note: A non-identifying cryptographic hash of your account ID is retained strictly to maintain anti-abuse protections and prevent unauthorized re-farming of promotional credits.
