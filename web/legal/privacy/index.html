Privacy Policy & Google API User Data Disclosure

How no5ocial accesses, utilizes, stores, and protects user data under our zero-knowledge, local-first architecture in full compliance with the Google API Services User Data Policy.

Last Updated: October 2026 | Effective Date: October 2026

Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance Statement

no5ocial's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

1 Overview & Application Purpose

no5ocial ("the Application", "we", "our") is a privacy-first multi-channel social media publishing and scheduling workspace. The application enables content creators, marketing teams, and businesses to compose, plan, preflight, schedule, and publish video and post content across multiple authorized platforms, including YouTube, Instagram, X (Twitter), and LinkedIn.

no5ocial operates on a zero-knowledge, local-first architecture. We do not operate centralized cloud servers that harvest, track, or mirror your personal drafts, credentials, or social media content.

2 Google User Data We Access & Collect

When you authenticate using Google Sign-In or connect your YouTube channel, no5ocial requests only the least-privileged OAuth 2.0 scopes necessary to provide core publishing services:

  • Basic Profile Information (openid, email, profile): Google User ID, Display Name, Email Address, and Profile Avatar URL. This data is used solely to establish and display your authenticated workspace session.
  • YouTube Data API (youtube.upload, youtube.readonly, youtube.force-ssl): When you explicitly connect YouTube as a destination channel, no5ocial accesses channel metadata (Channel Name, Channel ID, Thumbnail) and requests upload permissions strictly to publish videos, descriptions, tags, and thumbnails on your behalf.
  • What We Never Access: no5ocial never requests, reads, or stores your private Google Drive documents, Gmail messages, contacts, payment methods, browsing history, or search logs.

3 How We Use Google User Data

no5ocial uses information received from Google APIs strictly for the following operational purposes:

  • Workspace Authentication: To verify your identity and protect access to your local workspace session.
  • Channel Integration & Publishing: To query your authorized YouTube channel identifiers, upload video assets, set publication schedules, apply titles/tags, and monitor delivery status per your explicit direction.
  • Quota & Preflight Validation: To inspect channel status and quota availability to prevent failed publication attempts.

We never use your Google user data for background surveillance, undisclosed telemetry, or automated profiling.

4 Google Limited Use Requirements

In accordance with the Google API Services User Data Policy:

  • We only use Google data to provide and improve user-facing features that are prominent in the no5ocial user interface.
  • We do not transfer Google user data to external parties unless necessary to provide or improve these user-facing features, comply with applicable law, or as part of a merger/acquisition with user consent.
  • We do not use or transfer Google user data to serve advertisements, including personalized, re-targeted, or interest-based advertising.
  • We do not allow humans to read Google user data unless you have given explicit affirmative consent, it is necessary for security purposes (such as investigating a bug or abuse), or it is required to comply with applicable law.

5 Strict Prohibition on Data Sale & Third-Party Sharing

no5ocial maintains an uncompromising NO-SALE policy:

  • We NEVER sell, rent, lease, trade, or monetize your Google user data, YouTube content, or personal identifiers to third parties.
  • We NEVER share your Google user data with data brokers, ad networks, behavioral tracking firms, or data analytics aggregators.
  • All communication with Google APIs occurs over secure, encrypted TLS/HTTPS connections directly between your client device and Google servers.

6 No Artificial Intelligence (AI) Model Training

Your Google user data, video uploads, metadata, post drafts, and captions are NEVER used to train, evaluate, fine-tune, or develop artificial intelligence (AI), machine learning (ML), or large language models (LLMs). Your creative work remains your intellectual property and is never harvested as training data.

7 Prohibition of AI NCII (Non-Consensual Intimate Imagery)

In strict accordance with the Google APIs Terms of Service and Developer Policies:

no5ocial does NOT generate, process, alter, distribute, or facilitate Non-Consensual Intimate Imagery (NCII), synthetic non-consensual sexual content, deepfakes, or sexually explicit material using Google APIs or across any connected platform. Accounts attempting to use no5ocial for unlawful, non-consensual, or sexually abusive material will be immediately rejected and reported.

8 Data Storage, Retention, and Security

  • 100% Local Storage: OAuth tokens, publishing queues, brand settings, and post drafts are saved strictly on your local device (browser IndexedDB / SQLite database). We operate zero remote backend database servers that cache your content or credentials.
  • Token Protection: Access and refresh tokens are isolated and handled through secure platform storage.
  • Retention: Data persists only for the duration you choose to retain it in your workspace. We do not maintain off-site backups or remote archives of your personal data.

9 User Control, Access Revocation, and Data Deletion

You retain complete sovereignty over your data and channel permissions at all times:

  • Disconnecting within no5ocial: Navigate to Workspace Settings → Connected Channels and click "Disconnect" on any channel to immediately purge associated tokens and metadata from your device.
  • Revoking Access via Google: You can revoke no5ocial's access to your Google Account at any time by visiting Google Account Security Settings: https://myaccount.google.com/permissions
  • Full Data Purge: You can delete all locally cached database entries, drafts, and settings with one click via Workspace Settings → Database Management → Reset Database, or by clearing your browser site data.

10 Third-Party Social Network Terms

By utilizing YouTube integrations via no5ocial, users are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. When connecting Instagram, X, or LinkedIn, users must comply with their respective Developer Terms and Privacy Policies.
